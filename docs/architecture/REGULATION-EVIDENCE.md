# BuildWise AI — Municipal Regulation Evidence

## Checklist
068 — Municipal Regulation Evidence

## Rule
BuildWise stores evidence and provenance separately from interpretation.

A source is usable for regulatory decision input only when:
- authority is recorded;
- source URL is recorded;
- status is verified;
- effective dates do not exclude the current date.

Unverified municipal information must never be presented as a verified regulatory fact.

## Evidence fields
jurisdiction, subject, authority, source title, source URL, issue/effective dates,
retrieval time, excerpt, document hash, status, interpretation and interpretation status.

## Acceptance
PARTIAL until representative official municipal documents are ingested, provenance is persisted,
and runtime/UI verification confirms the evidence boundary.
