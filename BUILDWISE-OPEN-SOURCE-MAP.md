# BuildWise AI — Open Source Reuse Map

Principle: inspect before rewriting. Verify license, security, maintenance, architecture and fit. Then REUSE, ADAPT, REFERENCE, BUILD or REJECT.

High-value GitHub references:
- datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP — BOQ, 4D/5D, CPM, EVM, procurement, finance, RFI, quality, HSE, KPI, CAD/BIM and modular architecture.
- MANO-PPL/MANO-ERP — project workspace, schedule/WBS, drawings/CAD, quality/safety, contracts, materials and financial valuations.
- ua5191086-boop/OpenConstructionERP — project/BOQ/contracts/finance/procurement/HSE/quality/risk/change/EVM, multi-currency and AI modules.
- iRaed/pm-os — modular property-management operating system.
- prolinkinfo/RealEstateCRM — MIT real-estate CRM reference.
- bohd4nx/app-landing — Next.js/Tailwind app landing.
- fudailzafar/structra — Next.js/React/Tailwind and Framer Motion landing patterns.
- mehulbhakuni/rizz — installable PWA patterns.

Mandatory checks before importing code:
1. License compatibility.
2. Security/auth/RLS.
3. Dependency/build footprint.
4. Supabase/Postgres compatibility.
5. Persian/RTL localization.
6. IRR/USD multi-currency.
7. Custom workflow/KPI/form configurability.
8. Mobile/PWA performance.
9. Tests and maintenance.

BuildWise-specific layers to preserve: real-estate graph, Iranian market data, custom workflows, AI decision layer, approval/audit, project accounting, builder sales and CRM integration.
